Clinical Validation & Pedagogical Explainer

Centrifugation & High-Abundance Depletion

To uncover low-concentration cardiac biomarkers shedding from ischemic tissue, plasma proteins like albumin and immunoglobulins must be fractionated. Without active depletion, these high-abundance matrix elements override detection instruments, hiding crucial trace biomarkers.
